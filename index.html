Common Etiquette Questions

Cultural customs that surprise travelers the most

Is tipping polite in Japan?

Tipping is not customary in Japan. Good service is simply the standard, so staff will often politely refuse or return a tip. It's less an insult than an unfamiliar, slightly awkward gesture — just say thank you instead.

Is thumbs up offensive in Iran?

Traditionally, yes — in Iran the thumbs up has long been an obscene gesture, roughly "up yours". Many younger, urban Iranians know its Western meaning, but a nod or a spoken thanks is the safer choice.

Is showing the soles of feet rude in Thailand?

Yes — pointing or showing the soles of your feet is considered very rude in Thailand. Feet are regarded as the lowest, most spiritually unclean part of the body.

Is direct eye contact rude in Japan?

Prolonged direct eye contact is often seen as rude or aggressive in Japan, especially toward superiors. Brief eye contact is acceptable.

Is it impolite to refuse food in India?

Refusing food offered by a host can be seen as impolite in India. Food is a gesture of love and hospitality.

Is punctuality important in Germany?

Punctuality is highly valued in Germany, especially in business. Arriving late without prior notice is considered disrespectful.
