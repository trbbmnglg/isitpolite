Privacy-first by design

Privacy Notice

IsItPolite.com is built to collect no personal data. This notice explains exactly what that means, in plain language. Last updated: 18 October 2026.

No accounts · No tracking · No personal data

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-ups, no names, no email addresses.
  • No tracking cookies, no advertising trackers, no analytics profiling.
  • Our host, Cloudflare, runs cookieless, aggregate page-view analytics and automated bot protection on every page. See "Cloudflare (hosting)" below.
  • We don't store your IP address. For abuse prevention it's briefly processed to compute a short-lived, irreversible rate-limit token (auto-deleted within minutes) — never linked to you or your lookups.
  • Your history, cached results, and optional home country stay on your device (browser localStorage). Only what a lookup needs is sent with it, and none of it is stored by us.
  • When you report, confirm, or send feedback, we store only an anonymous, AI-sanitized summary — never your identity.

What is sent to the AI service

To generate an etiquette answer, the gesture, country, and context you enter are sent to a third-party AI provider for processing. These are query terms, not personal data, and we do not attach any identifier to them. If you choose to set a home country, it is saved only in your browser and sent with each lookup so the answer can say what changes for someone from there; we do not store it. We ask you to avoid entering personal information in free-text fields; report and feedback text is automatically screened and stripped of any personal details before anything is stored.

Premium artifact generation (paid feature): when you generate an infographic, dossier, coaching card, certificate, or phrasebook, the etiquette data from your existing result is sent to a separate AI service for content authoring. The resulting content is then passed through Cloudflare Browser Rendering to produce the final image or PDF. Both of these are transient processors — they are used in-flight for the generation and do not retain your data after the request completes.

Premium payments

One-time credit packs ($10 / $15 / $20) are processed by PayPal. IsItPolite.com never sees or stores your payment details (card number, PayPal account, name). The only information we retain after a successful payment is:

  • A random, computer-generated unlock code (e.g. XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX).
  • Two credit counters (search and generation), decremented as you use each type.
  • An opaque PayPal capture ID — a random reference number, not linked to your identity.
  • A coarse day-level date.

Free usage (10 searches and 1 creation per day) is counted via the same short-lived, salted, irreversible token described above — never your IP address — and resets daily. No new personal data or identifier is introduced for free usage.

If you enter an email address to receive your unlock code, that email is passed once to our email delivery provider (Resend) and is never stored in our database. Unlock codes are stored in your browser's localStorage — we have no copy of which device or person uses a given code.

What we store (and what we don't)

Stored (in an anonymous community database):

  • A verdict key — the gesture, country, and context of a lookup (e.g. "tipping · Japan · dining").
  • Aggregate counts — how many people confirmed or corroborated-disputed a verdict.
  • An AI-sanitized, personal-data-free summary of an accepted report or feedback message.
  • A coarse, day-level date (no precise timestamps).
  • Aggregate daily counts of answered checks by mode (for example, "42 Check answers on 18 Oct 2026") — no identifiers of any kind.
  • A short-lived, salted rate-limit token (an irreversible hash — not your IP) used for both burst abuse prevention and free daily usage counting; auto-deletes within days.
  • For premium purchases: a random unlock code, two credit counters (search and generation), opaque PayPal capture ID, and coarse day (see "Premium payments" above).

Never stored: names, emails, phone numbers, payment details, IP addresses, device IDs, precise location, cookies, or any other identifier that could single you out.

How reports stay genuine (and abuse-resistant)

Every inaccuracy report is reviewed by an AI moderation agent that filters trolling, spam, and off-topic content, and removes any personal data. A verdict's accuracy only changes after several independent reports corroborate each other — a single submission (or a troll that slips through) never moves the number on its own.

Lawful basis & your rights

Where the GDPR and similar laws apply, our basis for processing is your consent (which you give before your first lookup, and again when you choose to submit a report or feedback) and our legitimate interest in preventing abuse and improving accuracy. Processing is limited to these purposes (purpose limitation) and to the minimum data needed (data minimization).

Because we hold no personal data and no identifiers, we have no way to link any stored summary back to you — so there is nothing personal to access, correct, export, or erase. The "Clear All" button in the History tab removes your saved lookup history; cached results, offline guides and any unlock code stay on your device until you clear this site's data in your browser settings. You may withdraw consent by not using the reporting/feedback features.

Retention & security

Anonymous community summaries and counts are retained to keep accuracy stats meaningful over time; because they contain no personal data, no personal-data retention limit applies. Data is hosted on Cloudflare's infrastructure and transmitted over HTTPS.

Children

The service is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any data from children — because it does not collect personal data from anyone.

Standards alignment — an honest note

This service is designed to align with the principles of the GDPR and ISO/IEC 27701 (privacy information management) and ISO/IEC 27001 (information security): data minimization, purpose limitation, transparency, consent, and security by design.

To be clear and not mislead you: IsItPolite.com is not formally "ISO certified" and makes no certification claim. ISO certification requires an independent audit of an organization by an accredited body. We describe alignment with these standards' principles — not a certification.

Changes & contact

If this notice changes, the "last updated" date above will change with it. Questions or privacy requests? Use the in-app feedback form (linked from the site footer) — it's anonymous, so please don't include personal details.
